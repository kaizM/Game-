# Working goal

Build Harborlight into a polished, original urban roleplay game with a large multiplayer world, and validate it in bounded steps.

## Mac workload constraint

- Keep development on this Mac for now.
- Keep Unity closed during source, documentation, and asset review work.
- Avoid repeated screenshots and avoid running OneState alongside Unity.
- Batch Unity work into a short, necessary build or verification session, then close the editor/player.
- If the Mac becomes hot or heavily loaded, stop the Unity session and continue with lightweight work after it cools.
- Do not claim a build or visual change is verified unless a bounded check actually completed.
