# Asset register index

The canonical source/creator/license/acquisition/modification register is [`Assets/Art/THIRD-PARTY-ASSET-REGISTER.md`](../Assets/Art/THIRD-PARTY-ASSET-REGISTER.md). Original visual intent and prototype limitations are in [`ART-DIRECTION.md`](../ART-DIRECTION.md) and [`ART-AND-CONTENT-GUIDE.md`](../ART-AND-CONTENT-GUIDE.md).

Current third-party assets include the Quaternius Cars Pack and Universal Animation Library under CC0, MakeHuman Community character assets with CC0 notices, Poly Haven textures and modular building facades under CC0, and three Quaternius Downtown City building shells under CC0. Keep license notices and per-asset acquisition records in the project; raw archives are optional when imported source assets are already included, and archives over GitHub's file-size limit are excluded from the repository. The Downtown City shells now add varied geometry to streamed outer districts; interiors, block density, terrain, character, vehicle, and prop art still need production work.

Never add OneState assets, extracted scenes/maps, code, UI, audio, names, or protected content to this project.
